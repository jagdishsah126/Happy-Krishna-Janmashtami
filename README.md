## 🌌 Overview

> *"The Bhagavad Gita was not spoken in a quiet temple. It was spoken on a chaotic battlefield to a man who had completely lost his way. If you are feeling lost, confused, or terrified of the future, you are in the exact right place to begin."*

**The Bhagavad Gita Meditative Sanctuary** is a peaceful, client-side spiritual web experience created for **Krishna Janmashtami**. Designed specifically for anyone carrying anxiety about their career, relationships, or the future, it provides a quiet sanctuary to drop your shoulders, breathe deeply, and find timeless solace through the core yogic teachings of Shri Krishna.

## 📜 The 5 Sacred Gateways of Peace

The sanctuary guides you through five sequential psychological and spiritual gateways:

1. **🕊️ Gateway I: The Overwhelm (*Vishada Yoga • BG 2.7*)**  
   *Verse*: *"My heart is weighed down with pity; my mind is confused about my duty..."*  
   *Essence*: Normalizes feeling lost. Gives you permission to pause and freeze; acknowledges that confusion is the sacred space before clarity.

2. **⚖️ Gateway II: The Illusion of Control (*Karma Yoga • BG 2.47*)**  
   *Verse*: *"You have a right to perform your prescribed duty, but not to the fruits of action..."*  
   *Essence*: Relieves future anxiety. Drops the heavy burden of "what ifs" and centers your mind on today's honest effort.

3. **🧘 Gateway III: The Anchor of the Mind (*Dhyana Yoga • BG 6.35*)**  
   *Verse*: *"The mind is restless and difficult to restrain, but it is subdued by practice and detachment."*  
   *Essence*: Quiets the internal storm. Teaches gentle forgiveness for wandering thoughts and repeatedly returning to the "now".

4. **🧭 Gateway IV: The Path Forward (*Svadharma • BG 3.35*)**  
   *Verse*: *"It is far better to discharge one's prescribed duties, even though faultily, than another's duties perfectly."*  
   *Essence*: Freedom from comparison. Walking your own authentic truth without needing a 10-year master plan.

5. **🪷 Gateway V: The Sacred Refuge (*Bhakti & Sharanagati • BG 18.66*)**  
   *Verse*: *"Abandon all varieties of religion and just surrender unto Me... Do not fear."*  
   *Essence*: The ultimate sigh of relief. Total surrender to the divine flow of life and release of fear.

6. **✨ Final Sanctuary: The Diya of Surrender (*BG 6.40*)**  
   *Interactive Ritual*: Type any worry or choose a preset intention. Clicking **Release into the Sky 🪔** launches a dedicated floating Diya carrying your written prayer ribbon into the cosmos.

## 🛠️ How to Customize Content

All wisdom texts, Sanskrit shlokas, and translations are separated cleanly in the `data/` folder.  
To edit or add new reflections:
1. Open any file in `data/` (e.g., [`data/chapter1.js`](data/chapter1.js)).
2. Edit the text fields (quotes, explanations, takeaways, or mantras).
3. Save the file — the changes automatically render on the sanctuary!

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Clone or download the repository:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/jagdishsah126/Happy-Krishna-Janmashtami.git
   ```
2. Open [`index.html`](index.html) directly in any modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge).
3. No build step or local web server is required.
